Welcome to Lingofy ("we", "our", or "us"), owned and operated by Factictionary. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a secure learning experience. This Privacy Policy explains how our application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our mobile application and website at https://lingofy.factictionary.in.

🔒 Core Privacy Guarantee: Lingofy is designed with an on-device privacy-first architecture. Your spoken audio, voice recordings, translation inputs, and flashcard learning progress are processed locally on your device and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

1. Information We Collect

A. Information Processed Locally on Your Device

  • Audio & Speech Data: When you use the AI Pronunciation Coach or Live Speech Translator, microphone audio is converted to text and phonetic scores directly on your device. Audio recordings are not stored permanently or uploaded to our servers.
  • Learning Progress & Profile: XP, gems, hearts, daily streak counts, completed lessons, and unlocked achievements are stored locally in on-device storage (Hive NoSQL database).

B. Information Collected Automatically by Third-Party Services

To support free features and in-app monetization, our app integrates trusted third-party SDKs that may collect certain device identifiers:

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK): May collect device identifiers, Android Advertising ID (AAID / AD_ID), IP address, and general device telemetry to deliver frequency-capped, non-fraudulent, and contextual or personalized advertisements in accordance with Google's Advertising Policies.
  • Google Play In-App Billing: When making in-app purchases or subscriptions, Google Play handles payment card details and transaction billing securely. We only receive purchase tokens and entitlement status (e.g. VIP Pass active) from Google Play.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide offline interactive language lessons, pronunciation feedback, and two-way speech translation.
  • To track your gamified streaks, badges, and learning milestones.
  • To authenticate and unlock purchased language packs, combos, and VIP Pro subscriptions.
  • To serve relevant, policy-compliant advertisements via Google AdMob to support free access.

3. Device Permissions & Why We Need Them

  • Microphone (`RECORD_AUDIO`): Required solely for real-time speech-to-text recognition in the Pronunciation Coach and Live Voice Translator. Audio is processed on-device in real-time.
  • Google Advertising ID (`AD_ID`): Declared in compliance with Android 13+ requirements for Google Mobile Ads delivery and fraud prevention.
  • Internet Access (`INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`): Used to load AdMob advertisements, verify Google Play In-App Purchases, and fetch optional external neural TTS voices if online. Core translation and flashcards operate 100% offline.

4. Data Retention & Deletion

Because your learning data and profile are stored locally on your device, you have complete control over your data. You can erase all learning progress, history, and cached data at any time by clearing the application data in your Android device settings or by uninstalling the app.

5. Children's Privacy

Lingofy is built for all ages and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play Families Policies. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Third-party ad networks are configured to adhere to child-directed ad standards where applicable.
